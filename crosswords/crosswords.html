Crosswords

I'm passionate about crosswords, and I created this section to share puzzles that I've designed myself. I aimed to match the aesthetic standards of Settimana Enigmistica, a well-known Italian puzzle magazine. Creating engaging word intersections required extensive research, both to avoid overly simple words and to ensure interesting crossings. For this reason, all the crosswords are in Italian, my native language.

To let you play them, I built these interactive web pages myself with the help of AI. I really enjoyed taking on this coding challenge, and I hope you like the result and have fun in solving them!
